## Artifacts and limits

- Complete original prompts and evidence order remain in Renku `outputs/*/traces.jsonl`.
- Exported `audit.jsonl` files retain answers, calls, model outputs and sentence sources.
- `protocol.json` records model revision, packages, dataset and cohort hashes, and settings.
- A larger generator changes model capacity and cost; its gain is not evidence for the critic.
- Adaptive arms use the same checkpoint for critic and reader. Switching 7B to 14B changes both roles, so that comparison does not isolate reader capacity from controller behavior.
- Oracle gold paragraphs are an evaluation-only reference, not a mathematical maximum. Removing distractors or changing order can also change answers.
- The critic is prompted, not learned or calibrated. No train/dev threshold search was performed.
- A claim of novelty or superiority needs additional datasets, open-domain retrieval, matched compute and stronger external baselines.
